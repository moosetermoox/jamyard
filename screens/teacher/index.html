Before you project

One laptop: set your display to extend, not duplicate. Drag the projector tab (it opened in front of this one) onto the projector and keep this tab on your own screen. Nothing on this screen ever reaches the projector.

Laptop plus a phone or a second device: project the projector tab from the laptop. On the other device, open the link from Copy teacher link (bottom corner of the projector) and this console appears there too.
