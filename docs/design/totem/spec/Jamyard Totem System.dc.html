JAM YARD TOTEM
The Jamyard design system. Everything is an offcut: a piece of painted scrap wood, cut a little off-square, stacked with its neighbors into totems. Stacks mean sequence — rounds in an activity, steps in an editor, piles in a library — and the interface literally builds as you use it. Applied in 8a, 9a, 9d, 9g.

3 · MATERIAL — HOW AN OFFCUT IS MADE
Grain
Two repeating-linear-gradients at 92° and 88° (1px/6px and 2px/14px). Goes on every wood tone. Paint colors get no grain except yellow, which is painted thin.
The cut
clip-path polygons, 4–8 vertices, every edge within 8% of square — sawed, never torn. Rotation ±2° max. Notches and roof-cuts are allowed on feature pieces, one gimmick per piece.
The shadow
One flat drop-shadow(5–6px 8–10px 0 rgba(80,60,30,0.14)) on the whole stack's wrapper — pieces inside cast no shadow of their own. Free-standing paper: box-shadow 0 3–4px 5–6px rgba(50,35,15,0.2–0.3). Nothing blurs more than 6px.

GRAMMAR
A stack is a sequence read top-to-bottom or built bottom-up. Blocks overlap −4px, alternate rotation sign, vary width by 10–20%. Every stack stands on a plinth (walnut, ~60% width) and a base board (base brown, ~110% width) — a stack never floats. State on a block: yellow = current, 55% opacity + ✓ = done, wood = not yet. Hover pulls a block out sideways (±10px, away from its rotation); a dashed drop slot (3px dashed rgba(110,75,40,0.35) on a solid ground) marks where a new block can land. Cap: 6 blocks per stack — beyond that, split into piles like the library.

6 · MOTION
Interaction-only; nothing moves at rest. 120ms cubic-bezier(0.4,0,0.2,1) everywhere. Blocks in stacks slide out sideways ±10–12px on hover; free pieces lift −5px; press settles back to 0. New blocks arrive by dropping onto the stack (translateY −16px → 0, same curve, 180ms) — the one entrance animation. No fades, no scaling, no springs.
7 · NEVER
Serifs · torn or curved edges · border-radius (0 everywhere) · gradients except the grain · floating stacks (no plinth) · two red actions on one screen · body text on raw grain · rotation past 2° · blurry shadows · emoji
