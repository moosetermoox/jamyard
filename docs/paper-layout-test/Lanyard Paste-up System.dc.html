P A S T E U P
The Lanyard design system. Everything is a piece of paper cut clean and pasted on a gesso ground: no torn edges, no tape, no gradients. Paper and ink carry the interface; one magenta carries all the loudness; cyan and yellow exist only as misprint traces. Applied to the Home screen as 7a.

5 · MOTION
Interaction-only. Nothing moves at rest — no ambient jitter, no loops. Hover snaps the piece 2px toward its shadow; press lands it flat (shadow to zero). Timing is 90ms, steps(2, end) — a stop-motion frame click, never an ease. Screen transitions may hard-cut; fades are off-brand.
6 · NEVER
Torn edges or tape · gradients · rounded corners past 2px · a second loud color · misprint on body text · rotation past 2° · gray text lighter than #5C5546 · easing curves
