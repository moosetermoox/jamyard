Round 7 · Paste-up
Cut clean, printed loud
A fresh direction from the collage reference, replacing 5a. What carries over: grainy gesso ground, halftone dot fields, CMYK misprint offsets, magazine-clipping typography. What doesn't: torn edges and tape — every shape is a clean scissor cut with a hard paper shadow. Palette is mostly paper and ink with one loud magenta; cyan and yellow exist only as misprint traces and registration marks. Motion is snappy and interaction-only. Full recipe in the Paste-up system spec.

Round 6 · Library, in 5a's language
The shelf, and the shelf that talks
Both use 5a's material: speckled cream housing, one tinted translucent panel with a gloss curve, gumdrop radii, Garamond for names and pixel legends on buttons. They differ on how much browsing they ask for. 6a keeps the real structure from library.js — search, goal chips, Favorites and Recently used sections, Host as the primary action on every card. 6b takes the encounter literally: the panel asks what you want the class to do, then presents one activity at a time.

Round 4 · If Toby Fox made it
The whole interface is one dialogue box
Undertale's warmth is not in its pixels — it is in the writing and the pacing. The screen talks to you one line at a time, you pick from four fat options, and a red heart marks where you are. Almost no visual design is spent, so every ounce of personality has to come from the copy. That is a real strategy for an advisory tool: the product's job is starting a conversation, and this design starts one before the activity does.
4a Encounter Four options, one heart, all voice.

Round 3 · If Jony Ive made it in 1998
Translucent plastic, Bondi blue, gumdrop everything
The iMac G3 answer: tinted polycarbonate you can see into, a glossy highlight on every surface, fat organic radii, and light Garamond set with wide letterspacing. Nothing is flat and nothing is outlined — depth does the work. It is disarming rather than authoritative, which is a real answer to "trustworthy but not school-y". Type here is EB Garamond standing in for Apple Garamond, so this one steps outside the Campus type scale on purpose.
3a Bondi Ice-white housing, one tinted shell.

Round 2 · If Team Cherry made it
Hushed, hand-inked, lit by one lamp
Hollow Knight's calm comes from restraint: near-black ground, one warm light source, hairline rules, and enormous empty space. Applied here it stops being a tool and becomes a room you walk into. The dark version reads beautifully on a projector in a dim advisory room; 2b is the same language at dawn, for bright rooms and student phones. Illustration is left as placeholder — I can't draw the real thing, and this style lives or dies on hand-drawn art.
2a Lamplight One light source. Everything else recedes.
