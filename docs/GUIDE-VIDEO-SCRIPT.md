# The one-minute teacher guide (video script)

The written guide at `/guide` is folded now (the first five minutes open,
everything else behind a click), but the owner's goal is a one-minute
video that replaces most of the reading. This is the script and shot
list. Once recorded and uploaded to YouTube, paste the video id into
`GUIDE_VIDEO_ID` at the bottom of `screens/guide/index.html` and the
player appears above the text.

Target: 60 seconds, screen recording with voice, no music under the
voice. Record at 1920x1080 with the browser zoomed to 125% so the
projector text reads on a phone.

| Time | On screen | Voice |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00 | The yard (`/library`), cursor drifting over the shelf | "Jamyard runs whole-class activities: you project one screen, students join on their Chromebooks, and the AI does the busywork in between." |
| 0:08 | Click an activity, its popup opens, click Customize | "Pick one. Customize takes thirty seconds: say what you teach and it rewords the activity for your class." |
| 0:16 | The Make it yours dialog, class picker visible, one answer typed, Go | (no voice, let it work for two seconds) |
| 0:20 | Press Host. The projector screen with the room code. | "Press Host. This is what the class sees. Students go to the site, type the code and a first name. No accounts." |
| 0:30 | Split view: a Chromebook joining next to the projector, names appearing | "Names show up as they come in. When everyone's in, press Start." |
| 0:36 | A collect step running, then the continue button ("Send the question to students", "Reveal the results") | "From here you set the pace. Every button says what happens next, and nothing big appears until you press it." |
| 0:46 | Preview mode (`/prototype`), pretend students side by side, click a stop on the plan, Skip ahead | "Want to see it before class? Preview runs it with pretend students. Click any step on the plan to skip ahead to it." |
| 0:54 | The teacher console on a phone, hide a response | "A private console on your phone can hide any answer or remove a student." |
| 0:58 | Back to the yard | "That's it. Pick one and try it." |

Notes for recording:

- Use an activity with a fast payoff for the middle beats (Weekend Poem
  or Rose, Bud, Thorn). Preview with three pretend students.
- Keep the projector full screen in the projector beats; the corner
  brand mark is enough chrome.
- No student names in the recording: preview players are "Player 1",
  which is fine. If a real Chromebook joins, use a made-up first name.
- Captions: upload the voice lines above as the caption file; the
  script is already one sentence per beat.
